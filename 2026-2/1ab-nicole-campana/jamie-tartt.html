Jamie Tartt is one of AFC Richmond's most talented attacking players and wears the club's number 9 shirt. Born in Manchester, Jamie is a technically gifted striker known for his pace, finishing ability, close control and confidence in attacking situations.

A former Manchester City player, Tartt possesses the individual quality to change matches and has developed into one of Richmond's most dangerous attacking threats.

During his time at the club, Jamie has also evolved significantly as a teammate. Once known primarily for his individualism, he has gradually developed a greater understanding of teamwork, movement and responsibility, making him a more complete forward.
