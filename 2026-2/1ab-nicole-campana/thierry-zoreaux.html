Thierry Zoreaux is an experienced Canadian goalkeeper who has established himself as an important part of AFC Richmond's first-team squad. Born in Montréal, Zoreaux combines athleticism, confidence and strong reflexes between the posts.

After stepping into the side during an important match against Everton, he produced a clean sheet in a 1–0 Richmond victory and went on to become a regular presence in goal. His personality, communication and ability to perform under pressure have made him a popular figure inside the Richmond dressing room.

Before arriving in England, Zoreaux played in Major League Soccer with Montreal Impact. He wears the number 81 shirt for the Greyhounds.
